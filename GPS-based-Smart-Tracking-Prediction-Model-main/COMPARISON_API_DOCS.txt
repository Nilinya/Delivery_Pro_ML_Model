   - Use case: Cost vs speed trade-off analysis

   - X-axis: Weather Condition (Sunny, Cloudy, Fog, Stormy, Sandstorms, Windy)
   - Varies: Weatherconditions
   - Use case: Risk assessment and contingency planning
   - Business value: Identify high-variance scenarios, adjust SLAs

   - X-axis: Traffic Density (Low, Medium, High, Jam)
   - Varies: Road_traffic_density
